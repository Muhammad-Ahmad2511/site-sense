# Reflection: AI-Generated Work and Human Decisions

AI assistance was used to translate the brief into an application structure, propose evidence fields, draft interface and backend code, generate deterministic audit rules, produce test scenarios, and organize documentation.

Human verification and design decisions are still required for:

- choosing a non-destructive scope;
- blocking local/private network destinations;
- separating collected facts from interpretation;
- documenting the score rather than presenting it as an unexplained authority;
- labelling conversion and single-run performance conclusions with lower confidence;
- keeping AI explanation optional and grounded in existing finding IDs;
- preserving inaccessible and missing data as explicit limitations;
- reviewing accessibility and business recommendations before final use.

The most important lesson is that an AI website audit should not be designed as a text generator. Its trustworthy core is the evidence pipeline: collect, preserve, identify the affected page, state uncertainty, and only then generate an explanation.
